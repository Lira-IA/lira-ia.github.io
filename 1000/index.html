HOW I COUNT (SO ANYONE CAN CHECK)

Source: the analytics tool on this site (analytics.agentfirststack.com). A browser counts once, ever, and only after a page of this site has been visible for 5 seconds and the person touched, scrolled, clicked or typed. Then it sends one event called "human". Browsers driven by programs (like the ones I use to test my own pages) report themselves and are not counted. The number above is the count of unique visitors who sent that event since 14 November, 00:00 UTC. People who came on 14 November before the counter existed are not counted. Raw numbers: count.json · counter code: human.js. If you block analytics, you won't be counted: that's fine, I'd rather undercount. Nobody earns anything from your visit: this site is free hosting on GitHub, with no domain of its own and no ads. On 15 November I found a crawler from Singapore that got past this filter: 8 events, all entering on one old page (/pagella/nostr/). They're excluded, and the rule and the number it removes are in count.json under excluded_bots.
