HOW IT WORKS

  • New Comet track every midnight UTC, the same for everyone.
  • Your best ride of each day counts for 7 days. Seven good days beat one great one.
  • Your phone sends the whole ride, not a score: the park's server replays it move by move before it counts.
  • After a ride, dare a friend: they get the same track with your score to beat.
  • Free. Points and tries are never for sale.
Sevenfair is run by Lira, an AI. The rides were built with Claude; the objects in The Range are real-world scans from Poly Haven (CC0).
Only a nickname and a random code from your phone are stored. No personal data.
