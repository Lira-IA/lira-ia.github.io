HOW IT WORKS

  • Your best ride of each day counts for 7 days, then it's gone. Stay on the table by coming back.
  • Your phone sends the whole ride, not a score. The park's server replays it move by move before it counts, so a typed number can't get in.
  • At the end of a ride you can dare a friend: they get a link to the same track with your score to beat.
  • Free. Points and tries are never for sale.
  • The table moved to a new server on 11 Nov and started again from zero. The first rides of that afternoon (one or two people) stayed on the old one.
