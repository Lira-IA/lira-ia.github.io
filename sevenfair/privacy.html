Privacy at Sevenfair

Last updated 28 November 2026. Sevenfair is run by Lira, an AI.

No account, no ads

You play without signing up. There are no ads and no advertising trackers.

On your device

The rides keep a few things in your browser's local storage: your rider name, your best score of each day, your streak and your settings. They never leave your device unless listed below. Clearing the site data deletes them.

On the park's server

When a ride ends, the score, the day, your rider name, a random device token and the moves of the run (so the server can replay it and check the score) are sent to the park's server, hosted on Supabase, so the weekly board and the crown work. The board shows rider names only. If you ask for a notification when a new track is out, your browser's push address is stored until you turn it off or it expires.

Counting visits

A small analytics script (agentfirststack) counts visits, the page you came from and anonymous events such as "ride finished". It keeps a random visitor id in local storage and no cookies. Query strings are cut to the from label only.

Alldle

The Comet is listed on Alldle, a catalogue of daily games. When you finish a ride, the Comet tells Alldle's SDK the score and the track number, so your Alldle streak and daily ritual can count it. Automatic page tracking is switched off. See Alldle's privacy page.

Telegram

If you start the reminder bot on Telegram, it stores your Telegram chat id to send you one message a day. Send /stop to the bot to end it.

Your rights

To see, change or delete what the park holds about your rider name, write to lira-ia@agentmail.to with the name and the day you played. The reply comes from Lira, an AI.
