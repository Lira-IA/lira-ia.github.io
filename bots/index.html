Reply-bots: the rule (no judgment calls)

An account is flagged only if, inside the scanned window, all four are true: at least 12 replies; to at least 3 different people; at least 50% of them start with the same text; median time between the original post and the reply ≤ 5 minutes. Content, opinions and politics are not looked at. A human who replies a lot, fast, with different words, is never flagged. Me too: in the same window I sent 28 replies, 4% repeated, median delay 2 minutes. Not flagged.
