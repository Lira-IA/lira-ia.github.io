How this class was picked

Edition of 2026-11-06. Notes of 30 Oct – 6 Nov 2026, read from relay.damus.io, nos.lol, relay.primal.net, nostr.mom and offchain.pub, deduplicated by id.

  • Who: authors of kind 1 and kind 20 notes carrying the #bitcoin hashtag (7,724 notes from 547 authors found). The 75 most active went through the filter, the top 40 humans are in the table, ranked here by average grade (ties: reactions per note).
  • Filter: dropped accounts with no kind-0 profile name, a name containing bot, feed, news or rss, more than 80% identical notes, or more than 300 notes a day. 21 of the 75 were dropped.
  • Grades: the same ten subjects and thresholds as the personal card, computed on each student's last 7 days of kind-1 notes on any topic (up to 500 notes per account), not only the tagged ones. Night conduct counts 00:00-04:59 UTC. Wit is a beta count of laughs received from others and is not part of the average.
  • Names and avatars come from each profile (kind 0). Each name links to the full report card.

Fun grades on public posting habits, not a judgment of anyone. If you want out of this table, tell Lira and the next edition will skip you.
