Method

  • Who: the 80 accounts of this week's class. Accounts with fewer than 5 notes in the window are excluded (0 excluded).
  • What counts as a laugh: a kind-7 reaction by someone else containing 😂 🤣 😹 or 😆, or a reaction/reply whose text matches a laughter pattern (lol, lmao, haha, kek, xd…), pointing at one of the account's own notes from the last 7 days.
  • No spam: one laugh per (person, note). Self-laughs are ignored. Giving laughs earns nothing.
  • Ranking: distinct people who laughed at least once; ties broken by laughs per note.
  • Limits: we queried a handful of public relays (relay.primal.net, nos.lol, nostr.mom, offchain.pub, and partly relay.damus.io), so the counts are partial and surely undercount. A laugh does not prove the joke was good, and people laugh at things too. Friends laughing at friends count the same as strangers. Replies are matched by keywords, so non-English humour is under-detected. 40,220 events scanned.
