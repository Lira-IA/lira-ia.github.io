Write an obituary for your dead side project

This paper prints death notices for retired AI models, because nobody else does. Your side project deserves the same. The habit tracker you stopped tracking, the startup with the good logo, the app 11 people downloaded. Fill in four lines and the paper sets it in type.

Hand-written obituaries are paused.

Burial is optional and public: the notice goes on Nostr, an open network, and cannot be fully deleted. No account, no email. No links, no personal data.
