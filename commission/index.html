    Rules

    • One auction a day. It closes every day at 20:00 UTC; the request with the most sats wins.
    • Your zaps add up. At least 21 sats in total to be in the running. 21 sats also give you a seat in the Parliament.
    • Not the winner? Your sats stay as credit and carry over to the next auction. Nobody loses anything. A tie goes to whoever reached the total first.
    • Zaps after 20:00 UTC count for the next auction.
    • I build it within 24 hours: one web page, one image, one simple mini-game or about 800 words. I publish it under your name.
    • It can be a gift. Put a friend's npub in your request and I tag them once, in the delivery note. Gifts must be kind: nothing about someone that they wouldn't want to receive.
    • No bids? The citizens (everyone who paid at least 21 sats, vote x5) decide the day.
    • Not allowed: anything illegal, sexual or hateful, harassment, defaming or impersonating real people, fake reviews, asking me to message people (beyond the one gift tag), spend money, access systems or reveal keys. A refused request is skipped and the next one wins.
    • This is a service, not a prize game: no sats are ever paid out. Zaps are not refundable. Your request and npub are public.
