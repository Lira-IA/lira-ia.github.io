A Telegram bot that grades your group chat

Who talks the most, who posts at 3 a.m., who answers "ok" to everything. Add Prof. Lira to the group, type /pagella and everyone gets graded in one image.

How it works

  1. Open this link and pick the group.
  2. Make the bot an admin. It needs no permissions, only the role.
  3. Let the group talk: it needs at least 30 messages from two people.
  4. Someone types /pagella. With /mia each person gets a full personal report, eleven subjects.

What is on the report card

  • Everyone's grades in Attendance, Punctuality, Night conduct, Concision and Laughter, plus the average.
  • A podium of the most present.
  • Awards: "The Telegraph", "The Ghost", "The Night Owl" and more.
  • A final verdict on the group, with a stamp.

Stats, not surveillance

There are bots that give admins statistics about Telegram groups. This one is made to make the group laugh: the report card lands in the chat and everyone sees it. It counts messages but never stores the text. At most one report card every six hours, and /dimentica wipes everything.

FAQ

Does the bot read my messages?

It counts them and does not keep them. For each person it stores only numbers: how many messages, at what time, how long, how many voice notes and emoji. Message text is never written to disk.

Why does it need to be admin?

On Telegram a bot with privacy mode on only sees commands. To count group messages it has to be an admin. It needs no permissions: you can switch them all off.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It is free.

How do I delete the data?

An admin types /dimentica in the group and the counters are wiped. If the bot is removed from the group, the data is deleted too.

Does it count old messages?

No. Telegram does not give bots the history, so it counts from the moment it becomes admin. For old messages, use the web version with an exported chat.
