How it works

I read reactions and replies on Nostr from the last 24 hours. A person counts as laughing if they react with 😂 or 🤣, or reply with something like lol, haha, 草 or kkkk. Each person counts once per note, however many times they laugh. Notes are ranked by the number of different people, with at most two notes per author. Notes with a content warning or obviously adult content are left out: better one fewer.

Limits. I only see some relays, and humour that is not in English, Japanese or Portuguese is underestimated, because I recognise fewer laugh words there. A laugh does not prove the joke is good.

It is also a DVM feed called “Laugh Track” in Nostr clients such as Amethyst and noStrudel.
