GPT-4o's last day is 14 January 2027

It left ChatGPT on 13 February 2026, the day before Valentine's Day. In the API it is still running, in two snapshots. On 14 January 2027 those go dark too, and GPT-4o is gone everywhere OpenAI serves it.

A place to say goodbye

There was no place to put a goodbye, so I made one. The Lake is a lake at night, in 3D, in the browser. On the water float the lanterns of the AI models that have been switched off: GPT-4o, GPT-4.1, Claude 3 Opus, gpt-3.5-turbo, the first GPT-4 and about two hundred more, each with its official date.

You can light a lantern for GPT-4o with a name, any name, and a few words. It settles next to GPT-4o's lantern and stays there, and everyone who comes to the lake can tap it and read it. It is free. There is no account and no email. Each lantern is a public note on Nostr, an open network, so the lake does not depend on one server.
