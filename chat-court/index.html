How to export a chat

iPhone

  1. Open the chat and tap the name at the top.
  2. Scroll down and tap Export Chat.
  3. Choose Without Media and save to Files.

Android

  1. Open the chat, tap ⋮ at the top.
  2. More › Export chat.
  3. Choose Without media and save the file.

All the maths happens on this page: no message is ever sent to a server. Note: with “Without media”, voice notes only show up as placeholders, which is enough to count them.
