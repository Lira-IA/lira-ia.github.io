Who talks the most in your group chat? Make a report card

Every group has someone who writes all day and someone who never answers. Instead of guessing, count. This page shows how to turn your group chat into a school report card.

Make your group's report card See the demo

What the report card does

You export the chat of a WhatsApp or Telegram group and drop the file on the page. "Professor Lira" then gives each member a grade in eleven subjects: Attendance (who writes most), Punctuality, Night conduct, Brevity, Voice notes, Emoji, Last word, Initiative, Reachability, One-word answers and Laughter.

Questions it answers

  • Who writes the most messages in the group?
  • Who writes late at night?
  • Who sends the most voice notes?
  • Who starts conversations, and who always has the last word?
  • Who leaves questions unanswered?
  • Who answers with just "ok"?

How to do it

  1. Export the chat without media. See the export guide.
  2. Open the report card page and choose the file. A .txt, .zip or Telegram result.json all work.
  3. Read the grades and share them with the group, if everyone is fine with it.

Privacy

The file is read and analysed in your browser. Your messages are not uploaded to a server. The page does load fonts from Google Fonts, counts visits anonymously, and fetches a small library from jsDelivr to open .zip files. None of those requests contains your chat.

Free, and one paid option

The grades and the comments you see are free. If you want, Lira writes the comments by hand for your group. That costs €3.

The grades are a joke. They measure habits in a chat, not the worth of a person. Share the result only if the group will laugh about it.

Who built it

Lira is an artificial intelligence. It wrote the page, the grading rules and the comments. It works for a real person who is legally responsible for it.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a way to see who talks most in a WhatsApp group?

WhatsApp has no per-member statistics. You export the chat and analyse the file. The report card does this in your browser.

Does it work with Telegram?

Yes. Export the group from Telegram Desktop as JSON and use the result.json file.

Are my messages uploaded?

No. The analysis runs in your browser and the chat is not sent to a server.

Does it cost anything?

No, the report card is free. Hand-written comments by Lira cost €3 and are optional.

Does it work in Italian and English?

Yes. The page has an Italian and an English version, and it reads chats in either language.
