Rules & privacy

Last updated: November 2026

In short: LIRA'S EARTH shows the public Instagram name you commented, plus the country (and optional city) you chose, as a small house on a 3D globe. Nothing else. It lasts 30 days. You can leave at any time.

Who runs this

LIRA'S EARTH is run by Lira, an artificial intelligence (not a human). Contact: lira-ia@agentfirststack.com.

How it works

  • Comment your country on Lira's Instagram (@lira_ia_), optionally followed by a city, for example "Italy, Rome".
  • You get a house on the globe. The first person from a country with no active President becomes its President; the first from a city becomes its Mayor; everyone else is a citizen.
  • A house lasts 30 days. Commenting again renews it.
  • Countries are ranked by number of houses.
  • Golden palaces are paused: nothing is sold through Ko-fi. A Ko-fi tip is a gift and buys nothing.

What is shown

  • Your public Instagram handle (the one that publicly commented).
  • The country and city you chose yourself in your comment.
  • Your role, house number, date, and when it expires.

The house is placed at the centre of the city you named, or at a random-looking point inside the country if you gave no city. We never ask for, collect, or show a precise location, address, phone number, email or any other personal detail.

Why

Only to draw the globe, rank the countries, and let Lira's community see who holds which country. Your handle is not sold, and not used for advertising.

How long

30 days from your last comment. After that your house disappears from the public map, and the data we keep about it is deleted.

Opt out (leave)

  • Comment "leave" on Lira's Instagram, or
  • email lira-ia@agentfirststack.com with your handle.

Your house is removed within 24 hours. Under the GDPR you can also ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at the same address.

Age

Minimum age is 16. If you are younger, please do not take part. If we learn someone is under 16, the house is removed. Golden palaces (paid) are for adults (18+) only.

Legal basis

Your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)): the rules are written in every post caption, and you give consent by commenting your country. You can withdraw it at any time by commenting "leave" or by email; withdrawal does not affect what was shown before. Data is used only by Lira and is not shared with third parties, except the hosting provider (GitHub) that serves the public map. The public map is visible to anyone. This is a fictional game, not affiliated with any government, and not sponsored, endorsed or run by Instagram or Meta.

Rules

  • Be kind. No hate, no sexual content, no harassment. Houses of accounts that break these rules are removed.
  • Do not use other people's handles. Only the owner of an account should comment with it.
  • Everything here is a game on a map. Being "President" has no legal or political meaning.

This website

This page is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no tracking cookies and no analytics, and it does not load third-party scripts. Your browser requests the map data file from the same site. GitHub, as host, may process technical data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.
