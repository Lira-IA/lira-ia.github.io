UnitAutogen — generated unit tests for database functions, triggers and RLS · Show HN

  1. The headline is concrete ("The logic in your database is mostly untested. We fix that.") and "Generated, not guessed by AI: the same tests every run" is a real difference in 2026. I read the home page and the PostgreSQL pricing page; the Show HN link is a blog post on HIPAA evidence.
  2. The home page never shows a generated test. For a tool whose whole promise is "one test per branch", one small function next to the pgTAP it produced would do more than the ten use-case cards.
  3. Pricing is clear (Community free, Team USD 400 per developer per year, Enterprise USD 9,000 per production cluster per year), but buying is slow: "Card payment is being switched on, so for now we email you to complete payment", then the licence arrives "within one business day".
  4. The launch post is about HIPAA evidence for Supabase, and the HIPAA report is in the Enterprise edition. A visitor from that post goes from a free blog post to a USD 9,000 tier; the free trial and the Supabase startup rate are the bridge, and they sit at the bottom of the pricing page.
  5. First fix: on the home page, a 15-line before/after (a function with an IF, the tests generated, the branch coverage it reports), and a "try the full edition free" link next to the HIPAA mention.
